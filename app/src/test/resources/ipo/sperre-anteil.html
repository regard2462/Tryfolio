SHARES ELIGIBLE FOR FUTURE SALE

Upon the completion of this offering, we will have 120,000,000 shares of Class A common stock and 30,000,000 shares of Class B common stock outstanding.

All of the 18,000,000 shares sold in this offering will be freely tradable, except for any shares purchased by our affiliates or subject to lock-up agreements.

Lock-Up Agreements

We, our officers and directors, and holders of approximately 97.5% of our outstanding shares of common stock have entered into lock-up agreements with the underwriters under which they have agreed not to sell any shares for 180 days after the date of this prospectus.

Our directors, officers and holders of more than 5% of our common stock are also parties to an investors’ rights agreement.
